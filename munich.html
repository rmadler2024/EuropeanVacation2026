Featured Excursion – Neuschwanstein Castle

🏰 Neuschwanstein Castle

The highlight of your Munich stay. Visit King Ludwig II's fairy-tale castle near Füssen.

  • Tour the castle interior
  • Walk to Marienbrücke for the iconic panoramic view
  • Explore Hohenschwangau village
  • Allow a full day for travel and sightseeing
  • If needed, use the shuttle bus and request accessibility accommodations.
